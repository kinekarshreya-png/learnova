Daily study notifications

Reminders at 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM are shown while Learnova is open (a static website cannot wake itself when fully closed).

  1. Open Learnova from your home-screen icon (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen first; needs iOS 16.4+).
  2. Tap Enable notifications and press Allow.
  3. Tap Send test notification to confirm.
  4. If it says blocked: browser/phone Settings → Learnova (or this site) → Notifications → Allow.

The calendar option works even when Learnova is closed: open the downloaded file and tap Add in your Calendar app (10:00 AM and 8:00 PM every day).
