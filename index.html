Connection, sync and backup.

🔌 Connection

Your Cloudflare Worker. The Gemini key stays inside the Worker, never in this app.

Keeps strangers out of your AI and your synced data. Stored only in this browser and never included in backups.

☁ Sync laptop ⇄ phone

Sign in on each device with the same Worker URL and access code and your subjects, pages, notes, cards, quizzes and chat stay identical everywhere. Everything is encrypted in your browser before it is stored in your own Cloudflare account. Original photo/PDF files are not synced, only the text read from them.

💾 Backup & privacy

Browser storage can be cleared if you wipe site data. Export a backup regularly. Backups include text, notes, cards, chat and quiz history, but not the original photo/PDF files or your access code.

🔒 Private by design. Your data lives in this browser and, if sync is on, as encrypted data in your own Cloudflare storage. Only the text or images you choose to process are sent to Gemini through your own Worker. Nothing is public.
